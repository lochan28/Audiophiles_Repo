1 · Choose a voice

Every clip sits in a 3-second window. Shorter clips are placed inside it, and the SNR below is measured only over the part where someone is actually speaking — not over the silence.

2 · Pile on the noise

Each layer is set to its own SNR against the speech. Stacking layers therefore makes the mixture noisier than any single number suggests — four layers at 5 dB land near −1 dB, because noise powers add. The real combined figure is shown once you mix.
