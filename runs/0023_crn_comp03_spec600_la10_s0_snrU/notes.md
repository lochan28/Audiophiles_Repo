LOOKAHEAD 2 -> 10 frames, the architectural maximum. The encoder now sees 100 ms of the future instead of 20 ms, verified by perturbation: an output 10 frames before a changed input reacts, where previously only 2 did. Controls are runs 0016/0017/0018, identical but for lookahead. WHY: every knob inside the loss is now exhausted on both sides, and mask_diagnosis.py says the model hedges because it genuinely does not know which bins are speech -- correlation with the ideal mask is only 0.617 -- rather than because it is badly trained. Three attempts to punish the hedging out of it have failed. Lookahead is the first change that gives it information it has never had: whether a sudden loud thing is a plosive or a gunshot resolves in the following tens of milliseconds, and it was previously allowed 20 ms to decide. COST: total latency rises from 136 ms to 217 ms. Still well inside the 500-800 ms problem-statement budget, but it forfeits the claim of being inside ITU-T G.114's 150 ms comfort limit, which is a presentation claim rather than a requirement. Judge on all three metrics; a latency increase this large has to buy a clear gain to be worth adopting.
