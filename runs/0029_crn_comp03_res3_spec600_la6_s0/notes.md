MULTI-RESOLUTION SPECTRAL LOSS. The compressed-magnitude grader is now computed at three STFT window sizes (256/512/1024 samples = 16/32/64 ms) and AVERAGED, instead of at 512 alone. Controls are runs 0026/0027/0028, identical in every other respect and giving a three-seed noise floor of PESQ 0.0061, STOI 0.0004, SI-SNR 0.02 on this exam. WHY: a window length forces a trade between knowing WHEN something happened and WHAT PITCH it was. Our noise splits along exactly that line -- gunfire, mortar, rocket and artillery are bangs a few ms long, while helicopter, engine and ambient are steady hums. One 32 ms window sees each about half-well, so an onset smeared by 20 ms is an error the grader cannot currently see and never reports. VERIFIED BEFORE LAUNCH: with resolutions=(512,) the new code reproduces the old loss to 1e-9, and the three-lens average is 1.021x the single-lens value, so SPECTRAL_LOSS_WEIGHT=600 still means what it meant and this is a purely additive change rather than a disguised weight increase.
